# Game numbers

## Exam / work logic

### Exam pass mark

- 5 questions per exam (all exams)
- 4-5 out of 5 questions correct: PASS!
- 1-3 out of 5 questions correct: FAIL!

### Tasks per work shift

- 5 tasks per shift (all jobs)
- 3-5 out of 5 tasks correct: Good!
- 0-2 out of 5 tasks correct: Warning!
- 2 Warnings: Fired!
- Warnings reset after 5 days.

## Time

### Standard time per day

12 hours

### Extra time per housing tier

2 extra hours per upgrade

- Tent: 12 h
- Dorm: 14 h
- Rooming house: 16 h
- Apartment: 18 h
- Penthouse: 20 h

### Exam time

- Level 1 exam: 2 h
- Level 2 exam: 3 h
- Level 3 exam: 4 h

### Practice questions time

1 hour per question

### Work time

- IT help: 4 h
- Stud.ass: 4 h
- Office: 6 h
- Startup: 6 h
- Tech Corp: 8 h

- Level 1: 50
